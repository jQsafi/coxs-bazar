The Legacy

The Tale of Cox's Bazar

Known as the tourism capital of Bangladesh, Cox's Bazar boasts the world's longest unbroken natural sea beach stretching over 120 kilometers along the shimmering Bay of Bengal. This majestic coastal town owes its name to Captain Hiram Cox, an officer of the British East India Company. In the late 18th century, Captain Cox was appointed to oversee the rehabilitation of refugees. He established a bustling local market (a "bazar"), and to honor his compassionate administration and legacy, the town was officially named "Cox's Bazar" in 1799.

Today, its fame has transcended borders. From the stunning golden sands that meet turquoise waters, to the scenic Marine Drive flanked by lush green hills on one side and the roaring ocean on the other, the place has become an absolute paradise. Whether you are looking for thrilling water sports, mesmerizing sunsets, or the peaceful serenity of the ocean breeze, Cox's Bazar stands as a proud testament to natural beauty and historical resilience, beckoning millions of tourists every year to witness its undeniable charm.

Curated Journeys

Suggested Travel Itineraries

Handcrafted plans to help travelers experience the very best of Cox's Bazar's unbroken shores, scenic roads, and cultural sights.

Day 1

Arrival & Iconic Sunset at Laboni Beach

Arrive via the iconic Oyster Railway Station. Stroll the bustling Kolatoli & Laboni beaches, soak in the golden hour sunset over moon-shaped Sampan boats, and enjoy fresh fried Rupchanda fish for dinner at night beach stalls.

Oyster Station Sunset Watch Street Seafood
Day 2

Himchari Hilltop & Inani Coral Rocks

Climb the hilltop stairs of Himchari for a dramatic panoramic sea view and waterfall. Head south to Inani Beach to walk among ancient green-mossy coral boulders at low tide, returning for evening souvenir shopping at the Burmese Market.

Himchari View Inani Coral Stones Burmese Crafts
Day 1

Ocean Awakening & Surfing at Kolatoli

Morning sea bath along Kolatoli beach with beginner surfing lessons from local surf masters. Afternoon relaxation under beach umbrellas and a twilight walk watching local fishermen pull their nets.

Surfing Waves Sampan Fleet Grilled Lobster
Day 2

80km Marine Drive Open-Jeep Safari & Parasailing

Rent an open 4x4 Jeep and cruise the 80km Marine Drive between dramatic mountains and crashing waves. Experience thrilling tandem parasailing high above the ocean, with a sunset coffee stop at an oceanfront cafe.

Open Jeep Cruise Aerial Parasailing Marine Drive Cafe
Day 3

Maheshkhali Island & Shutki Heritage

Catch a morning speedboat across the channel to Maheshkhali Island. Visit the ancient Adinath Temple on Mainak Hill, Buddhist pagodas, and stop by the famous Shutki Mahal dried fish bazaar before heading home.

Adinath Temple Speedboat Channel Shutki Mahal
Days 1-2

Cox's Coastline, Marine Drive & Water Sports

Explore the best of Cox's Bazar main beaches, Himchari waterfalls, and Inani's coral boulders. Enjoy beach quad biking and high-speed boat wave blasting.

Marine Drive Beach Quad Bike Inani Corals
Days 3-4

Cruise to Saint Martin's Coral Island & Chera Dwip

Board the luxury sea cruise to Bangladesh's only coral island (Narikel Jinjira). Cycle along sandy trails lined with coconut palms, snorkel among live coral reefs, and take a local boat out to uninhabited Chera Dwip.

Sea Cruise Coral Snorkeling Coconut Palms
Day 5

Rakhine Cultural Trails & Farewell Sunset

Return to Cox's Bazar to explore Rakhine Buddhist monasteries, authentic handloom weaving factories, Burmese pearl markets, and a breathtaking farewell twilight beach stroll.

Rakhine Heritage Handloom Pearls Farewell Sunset
